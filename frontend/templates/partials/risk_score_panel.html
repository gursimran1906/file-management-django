How this is scored

Each answer carries a weight, shown beside the question. Adverse media about the client scores 12 where an unusual client scores 8, so the two count in proportion rather than being tallied.

Each section is capped, and a section that is serious on its own sets the rating on its own, whichever way the total goes. Sanctions, a designated person, a high-risk third country, a non-domestic PEP, proliferation financing and a transaction with no apparent purpose are high risk outright.

Totals then set the floor: under {{ breakdown.bands.medium_at }} is low, {{ breakdown.bands.medium_at }} to {{ breakdown.bands.high_at|add:"-1" }} medium, {{ breakdown.bands.high_at }} or more high. The overall rating is the higher of the two sides.
