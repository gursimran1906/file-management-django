How this is scored

Each answer carries a weight, shown in grey beside the question. The weight reflects how much that answer tells you: adverse media about the client scores 12, an unusual client 8, so the two count in proportion rather than simply being tallied.

Each section is capped, so no single run of answers can carry the whole rating.

A section that is serious on its own sets the rating on its own, at the points in the table below, whatever the total comes to. This is what stops a real risk being averaged away by a page of clean answers.

Some answers do not wait for a score. Sanctions, a designated person, a high-risk third country, a non-domestic PEP, proliferation financing and a transaction with no apparent purpose are high risk outright.

Totals then set the floor: under {{ breakdown.bands.medium_at }} is low, {{ breakdown.bands.medium_at }} to {{ breakdown.bands.high_at|add:"-1" }} medium, {{ breakdown.bands.high_at }} or more high. The overall rating is the higher of the client and matter sides.
